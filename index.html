2. Information we collect

NoomiClone does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, or other directly identifying details to play. The information involved is collected automatically by the Game and by the third-party services listed in Section 6, primarily to display ads, process purchases, and keep the Game working.

a) Information collected automatically

  • Advertising identifiers — Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android and the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS, where available and permitted.
  • Device and technical information — device model, operating system version, language, country/region, screen characteristics, network type, and similar diagnostic data.
  • Approximate location — coarse, IP-derived location (such as country), used by ad partners for ad delivery and legal compliance. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Ad interaction and performance data — events such as ad impressions, clicks, and rewarded-ad completions, used to serve and measure advertising.

b) In-app purchase information

If you buy an in-app product (for example, cosmetics, coins, or slow-motion), the purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play through Unity’s in-app purchasing service. We receive a purchase/transaction record confirming what was bought, but we do not receive or store your full payment-card details — those are handled by Apple or Google.

c) Information stored on your device

Your settings and game progress are stored locally on your device (using the platform’s standard on-device storage). This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

d) If you contact us

If you email us, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message, which we use only to respond to you.

4. Advertising and your consent choices

NoomiClone shows ads through a mediation platform that works with several advertising networks (see Section 6). Depending on your consent and device settings, ads may be personalized (based on advertising identifiers and interests) or non-personalized.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland. We use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (Google’s User Messaging Platform) compliant with the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF v2.2). When required, you will be shown a consent message before personalized ads are served, and your choices are passed to our advertising partners.
  • Apple App Tracking Transparency (iOS). On iOS, Apple requires your permission before the IDFA can be used for tracking. If you decline, ad partners will not use the IDFA to track you across apps, and you may receive non-personalized ads.
  • Resetting or limiting ad tracking. You can reset or limit your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy/Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and Apple Advertising).
