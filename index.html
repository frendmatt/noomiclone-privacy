Effective date: June 5, 2026

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Matthew Jones (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you play NoomiClone (the “Game”) on Android and iOS. The Game is supported by advertising and offers optional in-app purchases. By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Matthew Jones
Email: Noomiclonedev@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

NoomiClone does not require you to create an account or sign in, and you do not need to give us your name, email address, or other directly identifying details to play. Starting with version 2.3.0, you can choose to sign in to back up your progress. If you do, Unity gives the Game a player ID and your account’s email address, as described in Section 2(d). Apart from optional sign-in, the information involved is collected automatically by the Game and by the third-party services listed in Section 6, primarily to display ads, process purchases, and keep the Game working.

a) Information collected automatically

  • Advertising identifiers — Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android and the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS, where available and permitted.
  • Device and technical information — device model, operating system version, language, country/region, screen characteristics, network type, and similar diagnostic data.
  • Approximate location — coarse, IP-derived location (such as country), used by ad partners for ad delivery and legal compliance. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Ad interaction and performance data — events such as ad impressions, clicks, and rewarded-ad completions, used to serve and measure advertising.

b) In-app purchase information

If you buy an in-app product (for example, NoomiCoins or Remove Ads), the purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play through Unity’s in-app purchasing service. We receive a purchase/transaction record confirming what was bought, but we do not receive or store your full payment-card details — those are handled by Apple or Google.

c) Information stored on your device

Your settings and game progress are stored locally on your device (using the platform’s standard on-device storage). Your settings stay on your device and are not transmitted to us, whether or not you sign in. If you don’t sign in, your game progress also stays on your device and is not transmitted to us. If you sign in, your progress is also backed up to Unity Cloud Save, as described in Section 2(d).

d) Optional sign-in and cloud backup

Sign-in is available from version 2.3.0, in Settings > Account. It is optional: if you don’t sign in, nothing changes for you.

  • How sign-in works — Sign-in uses Unity Player Accounts. Unity’s sign-in page opens in your browser, where you sign in with an email address and password, or with Google or Apple. Unity handles your sign-in credentials; the Game never sees your password.
  • What the Game receives — Unity gives the Game a player ID and your account’s email address. The Game shows your email address in masked form (for example, “ja***@example.com”), stores only that masked form on your device, and never sends your email address anywhere.
  • What is backed up — Your regrabs, NoomiCoins balance, HQ replay credits, and the items you bought with NoomiCoins are backed up to Unity Cloud Save under your player ID. Purchases from the App Store or Google Play, such as Remove Ads, are not backed up: the store restores them on your device.
  • What stays on your device — Your settings, equipped skins, button layout, and language are not backed up and stay on your device only. The masked email address and the time of your last backup also stay on your device only. Your player ID is stored on your device too (Unity also has it, because your backup is stored under it).
  • Signing out — Signing out backs up your progress one last time and then removes it from your device. It stays in your cloud backup and comes back when you sign in again. If the backup can’t be made (for example, when you’re offline), you stay signed in. Purchases from the App Store or Google Play stay on your device.
  • Deleting your data — To delete your cloud backup or your Unity account, or to ask for a copy of your data, see Section 8.

Unity provides sign-in and cloud backup through Unity Player Accounts, Unity Authentication, and Unity Cloud Save. According to Unity, it collects the following information for these services and processes it under Unity’s privacy policy:

  • Linked to you — email address, other contact information, user ID, approximate location, customer-support information, and other usage data.
  • Not linked to you — crash data.

Unity says it uses this information to provide these services and, for some of it, for analytics, and that none of it is used for tracking.

e) If you contact us

If you email us, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message, which we use only to respond to you.

3. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game and its performance.
  • To display advertising, including, where permitted, personalized advertising, and to measure its effectiveness.
  • To process and validate in-app purchases and prevent fraud.
  • If you choose to sign in, to back up your progress so it is not lost if you reinstall the Game or change devices.
  • To diagnose technical issues and crashes.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. Advertising and your consent choices

NoomiClone shows ads through a mediation platform that works with several advertising networks (see Section 6). Depending on your consent and device settings, ads may be personalized (based on advertising identifiers and interests) or non-personalized.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland. We use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (Google’s User Messaging Platform) compliant with the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF v2.2). When required, you will be shown a consent message before personalized ads are served, and your choices are passed to our advertising partners.
  • Apple App Tracking Transparency (iOS). On iOS, Apple requires your permission before the IDFA can be used for tracking. If you decline, ad partners will not use the IDFA to track you across apps, and you may receive non-personalized ads.
  • Resetting or limiting ad tracking. You can reset or limit your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy/Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and Apple Advertising).

  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure, and improve the Game, serve non-personalized ads, and prevent fraud, balanced against your rights.

7. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Advertising and diagnostic data is retained by our third-party partners according to their own retention policies. Information stored locally on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the Game.

If you sign in, your cloud backup is kept until you delete your game account or ask us to delete it (see Section 8). Your Unity account, and the information Unity collects for sign-in and cloud backup (see Section 2(d)), are kept according to Unity’s privacy policy.

How to exercise your rights

Sign-in and cloud backup

If you signed in (see Section 2(d)):

  • Deleting your game account — See Deleting your NoomiClone account and data below.
  • Getting a copy of your game data — To ask for a copy of your cloud backup or other information we hold about you, email us at Noomiclonedev@gmail.com.
  • Deleting your Unity account — Your Unity account works across games, so deleting your game account does not delete it. You can delete your Unity account at player-account.unity.com/delete-account. The Game also links to this page.
  • Other Unity account requests — To ask Unity for a copy of your Unity account data, or for any other request about your Unity account, email Unity at unity-player-login-privacy@unity3d.com.

Deleting your NoomiClone account and data

This is how to delete the game account and cloud backup you created by signing in to NoomiClone (developer: Matthew Jones). You don’t need to reinstall the Game to ask for deletion.

  1. In the Game — Go to Settings > Account, tap “Delete game account”, then tap again to confirm. This deletes your cloud backup and the Game’s player record at Unity, removes your progress from your device, and signs you out.
  2. Without the Game — Email Noomiclonedev@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my NoomiClone account” and your Player ID. The Game shows it under Settings > Account > Player ID, with a Copy button. We can find your account only by its Player ID, because we never receive or store your full email address. We delete your game account and cloud backup within 30 days and reply to confirm.
  3. If you don’t have your Player ID — Delete your Unity account at player-account.unity.com/delete-account. After that, nobody can sign in to your game account again, and what is left of it (game progress stored under a random player ID) is no longer connected to you or your email address.

What is deleted: your cloud backup (regrabs, NoomiCoins, HQ replay credits, and items bought with NoomiCoins) and the Game’s player record at Unity. What is kept: we keep nothing else about your game account. Your Unity account is kept by Unity until you delete it on Unity’s page. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google, and advertising data by our advertising partners, under their own policies (see Section 6).
